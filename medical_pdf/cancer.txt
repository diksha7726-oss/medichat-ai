CANCER

Overview
Cancer is a group of diseases characterized by uncontrolled growth and spread of abnormal cells. If not controlled, cancer can invade surrounding tissues and spread to other parts of the body.

Cell Biology Background
Normal cells grow, divide, and die in an orderly manner. Cancer cells bypass normal regulatory mechanisms and continue dividing uncontrollably.
