LIVER DISEASE

Overview
Liver disease refers to conditions that damage the liver and impair its functions.

Liver Functions
Detoxification
Protein synthesis
Production of bile for digestion

Symptoms
Jaundice
Abdominal pain
Fatigue
Swelling in legs
Dark urine

Causes
Alcohol abuse
Hepatitis infection
Obesity
Fatty liver disease

Diagnosis
Liver function tests
Ultrasound
CT scan
Biopsy

Treatment
Lifestyle changes
Medications
Liver transplant in severe cases
