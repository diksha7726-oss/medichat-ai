ASTHMA

Overview
Asthma is a chronic inflammatory disease of the airways that causes breathing difficulty due to narrowing and swelling of the bronchial tubes.

Respiratory System Background
Air travels through the trachea into the bronchi and bronchioles, eventually reaching the lungs where oxygen exchange occurs. In asthma patients, these airways become inflamed and constricted.

Symptoms
Shortness of breath
Wheezing
Chest tightness
Persistent cough
Difficulty breathing during exercise

Triggers
Dust
Pollen
Smoke
Air pollution
Cold air
Respiratory infections

Diagnosis
Lung function tests
Spirometry
Allergy testing
Chest X-ray

Treatment
Inhaled corticosteroids
Bronchodilators
Rescue inhalers
Long-term control medications

Prevention
Avoid environmental triggers
Use prescribed inhalers
Maintain clean indoor environment
Regular medical checkups
