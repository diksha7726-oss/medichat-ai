COVID-19

Overview
COVID-19 is a contagious respiratory illness caused by the coronavirus SARS-CoV-2. The disease was first identified in 2019 and quickly spread worldwide, resulting in a global pandemic.

Virus Background
Coronaviruses are a group of viruses that can infect humans and animals. SARS-CoV-2 spreads primarily through respiratory droplets when an infected person coughs, sneezes, talks, or breathes.

Symptoms
Fever
Dry cough
Fatigue
Loss of taste or smell
Shortness of breath
Muscle aches
Sore throat
Headache
Congestion

Severe Symptoms
Difficulty breathing
Chest pain
Confusion
Bluish lips or face

Risk Factors
Older age
Chronic diseases such as diabetes and heart disease
Weak immune system
Obesity
Smoking

Transmission
COVID-19 spreads mainly through close contact with infected individuals. Virus particles can remain in the air in poorly ventilated spaces.

Diagnosis
PCR test
Rapid antigen test
Chest imaging
Blood oxygen monitoring

Treatment
Supportive care
Antiviral medications
Oxygen therapy
Hospitalization in severe cases
Intensive care support when necessary

Vaccination
Vaccines significantly reduce the risk of severe illness and hospitalization.

Prevention
Vaccination
Wearing masks in crowded places
Frequent hand washing
Maintaining physical distance
Good ventilation in indoor spaces

Long-Term Effects
Some individuals experience long COVID, where symptoms persist for weeks or months after infection.

Public Health Measures
Quarantine and isolation
Testing and contact tracing
Vaccination campaigns
Public health education
