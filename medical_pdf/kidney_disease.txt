KIDNEY DISEASE

Overview
Kidney disease occurs when the kidneys lose their ability to filter waste and excess fluids from the blood.

Kidney Function
Kidneys regulate electrolyte balance, remove waste products, and maintain blood pressure.

Symptoms
Swelling in legs
Fatigue
Nausea
Frequent urination
Loss of appetite

Causes
Diabetes
High blood pressure
Infections
Genetic disorders

Diagnosis
Blood tests
Urine tests
Ultrasound
Kidney biopsy

Treatment
Medications
Dialysis
Kidney transplant

Prevention
Maintain healthy blood pressure
Control diabetes
Healthy diet
Drink adequate water
