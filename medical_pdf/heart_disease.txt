HEART DISEASE

Overview
Heart disease refers to a group of disorders affecting the heart and blood vessels. It is one of the leading causes of death worldwide.

Human Heart Anatomy
The heart is a muscular organ responsible for pumping blood throughout the body. It has four chambers: two atria and two ventricles.

Heart attack
Heart failure
Arrhythmia
Congenital heart disease
